How it works
Current NFL: spin one of 32 teams using a 2026 preseason roster snapshot. All-Time: spin franchises and choose from legends. Eras: every spin gives you both a franchise and a decade. After a spin, tap a roster slot to open player cards: QB offers QB1/QB2, either RB slot offers RB1/RB2, and any WR slot offers WR1/WR2/WR3. In Blind, ratings and coach grades stay hidden until the season is over. Your final record is influenced by every player, the full offensive line, full defense, coaching, and some game-to-game randomness.
Unofficial fan-made game. Player OVRs and coach grades are original game ratings, not official NFL or Madden ratings. Current-player snapshot built for Aug. 13, 2026.
