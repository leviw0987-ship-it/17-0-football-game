How it works
Current NFL: spin one of 32 teams using a 2026 preseason roster snapshot. All-Time: spin franchises and choose from legends. Eras: every spin gives you both a franchise and a decade. A franchise can only be used once per run. Salary Cap gives you a pool-balanced budget — $120M Current, $130M Eras, or $200M All-Time — to fill all 10 roster spots. Better players cost more, premium positions such as QB cost extra, and the game protects enough minimum cap space for your remaining empty positions so you cannot accidentally make the roster impossible to finish. Normal lets you choose any empty roster slot. Competitive randomly offers only three empty slots after each spin. Hardcore forces a position group and depth card (for example WR3 or QB2), then you place that card into a compatible empty slot. In Blind, OVRs and coach grades stay hidden, but Salary Cap prices remain visible so you can manage your budget.
Unofficial fan-made game. Player OVRs, coach grades, and Salary Cap prices are original game values, not official NFL, Madden, or real contract figures. Build 2.6 adds the Phase 1 sports-card visual update. Current-player snapshot built for Aug. 13, 2026.
