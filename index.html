How the challenge works+
Current NFL: spin one of 32 teams using a Week 1 2026 roster snapshot. All-Time: spin franchises and choose from legends. Eras: every spin gives you both a franchise and a decade. A franchise can only be used once per run. Salary Cap uses pool-balanced budgets — $120M Current, $130M Eras, or $200M All-Time. Prices recalculate from each card's OVR, so roster and rating updates automatically flow into the cap economy. Normal allows any open slot, Competitive offers three, and Hardcore forces a depth card. Blind hides OVRs and coach grades until the season is simulated.
