How the challenge works+
Draft: build the same 10-slot roster across Current, All-Time, or Eras. Your Projected OVR updates with every pick and becomes your Final OVR when the roster is complete. You get one Draft Respin per run, and picks cannot be undone. Franchise: after the draft, spin a current NFL team to inherit its real 2026 17-game schedule, with one separate Branding Respin. Season: simulate week-by-week or finish the regular season automatically while the other 31 teams play in the background. Seven teams per conference make the playoffs, the #1 seeds receive byes, and surviving teams can reach the Super Bowl.
